Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Purl & Paper, called 织遇 in Simplified Chinese, for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: 13 September 2026

Information stored by the app

Purl & Paper stores the patterns, project details, yarn records, gauge swatches, measurements, photos, notes, counters, bookmarks, annotations and preferences that you choose to add. This information is stored in the app’s local storage on your device and is used to provide the app’s features.

The current version does not require an account, send your knitting library to the developer, or include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or a third-party crash-reporting service. Automatic library synchronisation through iCloud is not enabled.

Photos, camera and files

You choose which files and photos to import through Apple’s file and photo pickers. The app requests camera access only when you choose to scan a paper pattern. Imported copies and scans become part of your local library. You can review or revoke camera and photo access in your device’s Settings.

Exports and sharing

You can export a library archive or an annotated PDF and choose where it is saved or shared. The app does not automatically send an export to the developer. Library archives may contain original pattern files, photos, project and yarn details, swatch measurements, notes, counter history and annotation data.

Exports are not encrypted or password-protected by Purl & Paper. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read its contents. Store and share exports only in locations you trust.

Apple backups and external providers

Your device may include app data in an Apple or computer backup according to your settings. If you save an export to iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider processes the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight or diagnostic information according to your Apple account and device settings.

Retention, deletion and consent

Your library remains on your device until you delete individual records or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app can retain its data. Deleting an in-app record does not delete copies you previously exported, original files in Files, photos in your Photos library or older device backups; manage those copies where they are stored.

You can revoke camera or photo permission through the device’s Settings. Because the developer does not receive or maintain your local knitting library, the developer cannot retrieve, change or delete that library remotely.

Children

Purl & Paper is a general-audience utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not operate an account or data-collection service.

Website hosting

This policy and the support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit these pages under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The app does not embed this website or use it for analytics.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. Questions about privacy or support can be sent to goldidu1027@gmail.com.
