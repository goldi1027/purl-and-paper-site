A little help along the way

Purl & Paper Support

Help with patterns, projects, Apple Pencil annotations, counters, yarn records, gauge swatches and backups.

Start a project

Import a PDF, pattern photos or a camera scan, then save the pattern and create a project from it. The first page of the pattern appears as the project’s default cover until you add a cover or progress photo. Each project keeps its own counters, notes, progress and annotations.

Write on a pattern

Open an ongoing project on iPad and choose Write or Highlight. Ink options control colour and stroke size. Apple Pencil writes while your fingers move around the page; finger drawing can also be enabled. Saved annotations can be viewed on iPhone.

Rows, repeats and the guide

Row and repeat controls are independent. Use their plus and minus buttons manually, reset rows to zero when needed, and edit the repeat total. Drag the row guide to reposition it and use its resize control to make it narrower, wider, thinner or thicker.

Yarn and gauge swatches

Yarn Match compares a pattern’s recommended yarn with a stash yarn using the information you enter. A saved swatch can record its yarn, needle size, photo, measured dimensions, stitch count and row count. Gauge Check converts those measurements to a 10 cm gauge. A washed and measured swatch remains the final suitability check.

Back up your library

Open Settings and choose Export backup. Store the resulting .knitarchive file somewhere secure. Backups and annotated PDF exports are not encrypted by the app.
